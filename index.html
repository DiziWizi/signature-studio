Paste the HTML of a signature you already use. Bowerbird will try to recover the name, contact details, links and images. It’s best-effort — always review the result.

What recipients see

A signature is only one part of how you appear. These are the parts other systems decide.

Your picture in the inbox

Comes from the recipient’s address book, a company directory (internal only) or BIMI — never from the logo inside the signature. A vCard with a photo is the reliable cross-client route.

Your name before opening

The From display name is set in your mail account, not here. Use Export → Copy sender name & address to get the exact string.

Images

Proton and mailbox.org block remote images by default; Outlook asks once. Inline (CID) images survive where hosted URLs do not.

Dark mode

Apple Mail, Outlook and Gmail may invert colours. Dark-mode-aware templates keep contrast; check the preview in both.

Long messages

Gmail hides content past roughly 102 KB of HTML. The export menu shows your signature’s weight.

Verified logos

BIMI puts a logo beside the sender in Gmail, Apple Mail, Yahoo and Fastmail — it needs DNS records and a certificate. Microsoft does not display it.

Compatibility check

Scans the generated HTML for things Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail, Proton and mailbox.org handle differently. Everything runs locally.
